# Curated reference shots — described

19 Apple-style reference images (third-party Dribbble/Pinterest-type shots, collected
2026-08-26) that the field notes were derived from. The images themselves are **not** in this
repo (not ours to redistribute); these descriptions preserve what matters for design decisions:
proportions, tones, hierarchy. Where a shot contradicts a field-note rule, the field note wins
(noted per item).

---

### R1 — iOS widget gallery (grid of ~25 widgets on blue-grey)
Map "Parked Car", music "Cool / Dua Lipa", Today list on red, weather "Porto 29", stock
"AAPL 349.74", Activity rings, world clocks, calendar, Notes, "Daily Goal 8/16".
Takeaways: every widget = **one datum**; big number + small label everywhere ("8/16",
"349.74", "29"); squircle corners ~20–24px at widget scale; colour comes from content
(album art, photo, app tint) — chrome is white or black; small tinted title in the app's colour
(red "Today", pink "Dua Lipa") is the only accent; dense but never cluttered because each tile
has one idea.

### R2 — "Forma AI" pricing, black + blue glow
Pure black page; a giant "Pricing" word behind the cards, partly veiled by frosted glass cards;
blue light bloom behind. Three glass cards (hairline light border ~white/15, radius ~28px),
plan name 20px, price ~40px light-weight, 5 check rows with grey circular check badges, white
pill "Choose Plan" in all three; the **Pro** card is emphasised by a brighter glowing edge, not
a different colour. Glass nav pill at the top: items as text, the active one bright, one white
filled "Download" pill at the end. Toggle "Yearly" bottom-left.

### R3 — Monochrome pricing, black
Same structure without colour: giant white "Pricing" wordmark blurred behind glass cards.
Only the **middle** card's "Get Started" is a filled white pill; the other two are black pills
with a hairline. Hairline separator splits price header from features. Grey text body,
~13px. Confirms: *one filled button carries the hierarchy*.

### R4 — visionOS music app (Sia artist page)
Floating frosted-glass window over a softly blurred living room. Left sidebar list with the
selected row as a lighter glass capsule; hero card with photo cut-out, "Verified artist" small
blue check + grey text, name ~40px bold, monthly listeners grey. Green filled "Play" pill (the
one colour), glass "Follow" pill, text actions "Go to artist radio", "Share". Ornament-style
floating tab bar on the left edge (vertical glass capsule with icons) and a floating player bar
under the window. Takeaway: glass is for floating layers; colour only on the primary action.

### R5 — Frosted / Clear / Blur comparison
Three glass rounded rectangles (radius ~36px) over a photo: "Frosted" (heavy blur + grain +
slight tint, the most legible), "Clear" (iOS — nearly transparent, refraction), "Blur"
(One UI — blur, no grain). Large 60px white labels, tiny caption below. Use frosted when text
sits on busy imagery.

### R6 — Matcha waitlist (white)
Concentric faint green rings on white; tilted green speech bubble with white text as the hero;
circular avatar photos with white borders and soft shadows orbit around; 3D stickers (cake,
calendar, party popper). One black gradient pill "Join Waitlist ›" with deep soft shadow;
two underlined text links with small icons below it as secondary actions.
Takeaway: secondary = underlined plain text; one filled pill.

### R7 — "Gather here" (dark)
Near-black canvas; ~14 photo squircles (~120px, radius ~28%) laid out on an **arc** around a
centred message: 30px bold white title, grey 15px paragraph, small white pill "Join Gather".
Circular dark-grey arrow button on the right. Colour exists only inside the photos.

### R8 — "If you struggle to get X design clients" (light social graphic)
Light grey canvas with a faint white radial glow. **Two-tone headline** (dark grey → lighter
grey for "read this"), a black app-icon squircle **inline in the headline** with a coloured
glow under it. Small pill chip above with a hand emoji. Two white floating cards "Point A / Point
B" with icon squircles (one light, one black), dashed connectors down into an envelope
illustration holding cards "$0" and "$10.000+".

### R9 — AirPods Pro product page (white)
White rounded panel floating on light grey with very soft shadow. Huge faint grey "Air Pods
Pro" typography behind the product photo (background type device). Bold black stacked title
right of product, tiny grey paragraph, outlined grey pill "More". Vertical dot pagination on the
right. Product casts a real soft shadow.

### R10 — "Why Brands Keep Coming Back." (3 cards)
Light grey canvas. **Two-tone headline**: line 1 grey (#A0A0A0-ish), line 2 black, ~56px bold,
negative tracking, period at the end. Three white cards (radius ~24px, very soft shadow, no
border), each with a light-grey inner illustration well (inset radius ~18px) holding one 3D
monochrome object (dollar sign with ripple, black tablet with pencil, black circle with bolt),
then 20px semibold title + 15px grey two-line description. Everything monochrome.

### R11 — Profile card "Sienna Brooks" (white)
White outer card (radius ~32px) with photo inset (radius ~24px) — 16px white margin frame.
Name centred at the top of the photo, ~30px regular, "⟳ Connecting" small grey below. Footer:
small avatar with online dot, handle + "12m ago" grey, black pill "+ Add member".
Very soft wide shadow under the whole card.

### R12 — Status pills on black
Pill badges with coloured translucent fill + 1px lighter outline + matching icon: In progress
(cyan), To-do (indigo), In Review (yellow), Design Review (purple), Rework (red), Done (green),
Not Started (pink), Blocked (red), On Hold (blue), Archived (grey). Sentence case, ~18px.
Note: this is the *richest* status treatment in the set; the field notes restrict status colour
to dots/small tinted words in app chrome — use pills like these only for tag-like metadata.

### R13 — Mac mini "Climb in. Geek out." (Apple.com style)
Dark global nav bar (Apple menu items). Light blue-grey canvas with a grid of ghosted
neumorphic app-icon squircles fading into the background; one hero squircle tile (radius ~24%)
holding a glossy black Apple logo with deep soft shadow. Left: small "Mac Mini" label 20px,
headline ~48px bold two lines with periods, grey 13px paragraph. Classic Apple marketing
composition: left text block + right object, one vertical axis.

### R14 — AirPods Max product page
Very light grey canvas; product centred with soft shadow; enormous pale "Air Pods Max" text
running off both edges behind it. Top nav: text items, active one black, others grey.
Bottom bar: circular play button + "Play video", "01 / 04" counter (current dark, total grey),
outlined pill "More Product", two circular arrow buttons, black filled pill "Buy Product" at far
right — the single filled action.

### R15 — Pricing "Better tools smooth workflow" (light)
Light grey panel. Headline with mixed tones: most words mid-grey, key words black ("workflow",
"annually."), inline app icon squircles (Dribbble, Behance, …) floating with shadows inside the
sentence, and an inline gradient toggle switch. Small mono uppercase "PRICING" tag (the kind of
eyebrow the field notes **ban** — don't copy that part). Plan cards radius ~28px on #F7F7F7,
black icon squircle with shadow, price "$69" black + "/month" grey. Buttons are outlined wide
rectangles with two-tone labels ("Get **Started**"). "Schedule a **Call** ⟶" text link top right.

### R16 — Gradient "Get Started" buttons on black
Eight pill variants: frosted gradient fills (blue→white, mint→lilac, peach→blue), dark pills
with iridescent 1px borders, a dark pill with blurred aurora inside. Label 20px medium + paper
plane icon. Use sparingly — a single hero CTA on a dark marketing page, never in app chrome.

### R17 — Profile card "Marline Bettye" (photo full-bleed)
Photo fills the whole card (radius ~40px); name white ~40px at the top over the sky, spinner +
"Connecting" below; bottom gradient blend into the photo's dominant colour (olive); white
pill "+ Add Member" with shadow. Big coloured shadow under the card picks up the photo's tone.
Header chips: outlined pills "Daily Design Challenge" and "Day 6 / 23".

### R18 — iOS task app (three phones)
1) "New Task": circle close button (outlined), 34px bold title, segmented-like pills
"Today" (black filled) / "Tomorrow" (outlined), circular icon buttons, project chips
(one with a vivid gradient image fill), full-width black "Create" pill pinned to the bottom,
outlined pill text fields. 2) "Your Projects (4)" with count in lighter weight inside the
title, avatar right; project cards are full-bleed generative gradient images with 40px bold
white titles, "8/10 tasks" with a vertical capsule progress, circular glass buttons.
3) Detail: image header card, circle floating "+" overlapping the edge, task list with
**circle** checkmarks, hairline separators starting at the text, completed items struck
through and grey. (Uses a small uppercase "COMPLETED (2)" / "PROJECTS" label — **banned** in
field notes; replace with sentence-case titles.)

### R19 — Taskello card (warm off-white canvas)
Black squircle card with a thick black outer frame; top area a blurred red/orange photo with
the title right-aligned in white; a folder-tab-shaped dark grey panel below with "Daily memo"
white 18px + "Notes & Journaling" grey. Bottom: big "05" + small "Doc" (number+label pairing),
"1270 Notes" right. Canvas `#F3F2EA`-like warm off-white.
